About York Computer Help

York Computer Help started in 2007 when Nick was a young lad and started fixing and building computers for people in the local area. This work gathered momentum, reputation and the business expanded.

Now the team offers services to a wide customer base and delivers a much broader range of services. Our team features experience supporting home and business computer users from all skill and experience levels.

No matter what the issue, we're sure to be able to help!
